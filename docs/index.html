The Bonding

Privacy Policy  ·  NotCallCommonApps

Short version: The Bonding stores all of your data on your device only, encrypted with AES-256. Nothing is ever transmitted, collected, or shared. We have no access to your data — ever.

Effective date: May 17, 2026

What data the app stores

The Bonding stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your user profile
  • Partner records you create
  • Activity and event logs you enter
  • Health status records you enter
  • Lookup / reference values you configure
  • Your app password (as a cryptographic verifier — the password itself is never stored)

How your data is protected

All personal records are encrypted on-device using AES-256-CBC before being written to the local database. The encryption key is derived from your password using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and is held in memory only while the app is unlocked. Locking the app immediately clears the key from memory.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No data is transmitted to any server
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that send data externally

Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. There are no third parties involved. There is no backend, no cloud sync, and no account system.

Your control over your data

  • Export: You can export your data at any time from within the app.
  • Delete: You can clear all personal data or perform a full app reset from within the app. This permanently removes your data from the device.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the app removes the local database and all data stored by the app.

Android backups

Android's automatic backup system is disabled for this app. Your data is never included in device backups to Google Drive or any other backup service.

Children's privacy

The Bonding is intended for adults only (18+). We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
